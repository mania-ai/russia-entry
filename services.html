Questions

Before you ask

Do you take money from distributors or retailers?

No. No fees, no commission, no "success bonus" from the other side. If I did, I couldn't tell you honestly which partner is best for you.

Why "from"? What moves the price?

Number of categories, number of channels, how much data is public in your segment, and how many companies you want checked. After your brief you get a fixed price that doesn't change.

Will you sign our NDA?

Yes, happy to. Send it with your brief.

Do you import or distribute the goods yourself?

No. I don't buy, hold or resell products. That keeps me independent: I have nothing to sell you except advice and work.

What if Russia isn't a fit for us?

Then I'll say so, in writing, with the reasons. A clear "no" after a snapshot is cheaper than a failed launch.

What won't you do?

Anything that breaks sanctions or export rules, restricted goods, or hidden payment routes. If your product or setup is a problem, I'll tell you before we start.

Not sure where to start?

Send a short brief and pick "Not sure". I'll suggest the smallest useful first step.
