Whose side I'm on

I'm paid by you. Only by you.

Many "Russia consultants" also earn from the distributors they introduce. Then their job is to close a deal, any deal. I take no fees, commissions or favours from distributors, retailers or marketplaces. My job is to get you a deal that still works in year three.

  • Your trademark registered in Russia in your name before the first shipment, not the distributor's
  • Exclusivity only in exchange for volume commitments, with a clear way out
  • A price corridor that holds across retail chains and marketplaces
  • Marketing funds tied to proof of what was actually done
  • Payment terms and routes checked before you sign
Where it breaks

Where Russia entries usually go wrong

Foreign brands tend to find out about these after the contract is signed.

I help you see them before you commit money.

  • Retail margins that don't work at your recommended price
  • Marketing funds the distributor expects but you never budgeted
  • Certification that takes longer than the first shipment
  • Your product already on marketplaces through grey imports, at a price you didn't set
  • A distributor who registers your trademark in their own name
  • A distributor who signs, then sells your competitor first
